Rubik's Cube Solver & Tutorial

Drag to rotate. Pick a color below, then tap stickers (3D or flat view) to paint your pattern. Centers are fixed.
Arrange your own cube (flat view)
1) Pick a color above. 2) Tap stickers to paint every face to match your cube. 3) Press Solve.
Scan a scrambled cube from photos
1) Tap each box to add one photo per face (orientation under each box). 2) Tap a loaded photo to select it, then use the sliders so the 9 green circles sit on the 9 stickers. 3) Press Detect colors, check the flat view, then Solve.
Photo picker not opening? Send your 6 photos to Claude in the chat, and it will give you a code to paste here. You can also copy this cube's code.
